Printer acting up?

Here is what to do.

  1. Look at the printing page. A red note on a printer (“Paper jam”, “Out of paper”, “Switched off”) says what is wrong with it.
  2. Check the printer itself: paper in the tray, no jammed sheet, covers shut, no blinking error light, switched on.
  3. Waiting printouts are safe. They print once the printer is fixed. Anything stuck more than 30 minutes is cancelled by itself, so old pages don't come out hours later. You'll see “send it again”.
  4. In a hurry? Print on another printer: pick a different one on the printing page.
  5. Still stuck? Press Need help? on the printing page. It tells everyone with the page open.

Pages from different people never mix: each printer finishes one printout before starting the next.
